Project Overview

Scope summary, platform structure, and market context

📋
Requirement: A full Airbnb-style vacation rental marketplace — property listings, guest booking, host management, payments, reviews, and mobile apps (Android + iOS). Custom-built, three-phase delivery.
🔍
Budget Reality Check — Compared to Our Own Projects: Our Psyzen teaser site alone was ₹85K. Beats n Bells full event platform was ₹70–82K. Those are single-purpose apps. This is a two-sided marketplace — a guest platform, a host platform, an admin system, payment infrastructure, real-time chat, calendar engine, review system, and two native mobile apps. It is 15–20× more complex. The ₹18–24L quoted here is well within Indian market rate for this scope and is not inflated. If anything, it's lean for the deliverable list.

Revenue Model

Multiple monetisation streams — active from Day 1

Primary · Live from P1
Guest Service Fee
8–14% on top of listing price. Core Airbnb model. At ₹5K avg booking × 100 bookings/month = ₹70K+ platform revenue.
Primary · Live from P1
Host Commission
3–5% deducted from host payout. Combined with guest fee = 11–18% total margin per transaction.
Recurring · P2
Pro Host Subscription
₹499–₹2,999/month — priority listing, advanced analytics, multi-property tools. Predictable SaaS revenue.
High Margin · P2
Featured Listings
Hosts pay ₹999–₹4,999 for 7/15/30 day priority placement. Zero incremental cost to platform.
Second Vertical · P2
Experiences Commission
20% on experience bookings. Higher margin than stays — no property infrastructure.
B2B · P2+
Corporate Bookings
Corporate travel desk for company offsites, team retreats. Annual contract + fixed commission. Underserved in India.
